# Help improve first use

You can contribute by trying Loopset and explaining where it becomes confusing. No coding or simulation experience is required.

[Open the guided example →](https://berkebasbay.github.io/loopset/?learn=1) · [Send first-use feedback →](https://github.com/berkebasbay/loopset/issues/new?template=first_use_feedback.md)

Use only made-up data. Your projects stay in this browser; downloading project JSON gives you a portable copy.

## Try one task

| Task | Goal | A useful observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First example | Complete the guided help-desk lesson and keep your example as a template. | Where did you need to reread, search or ask for help? Could you explain the result in your own words? |
| Your own change | Open a starter, change one assumption and compare the result with its original plan. | Was it clear what changed, which calculation you were looking at, and what the result meant? |
| Empty site | Start with Blank, place a model, choose Start a process, and save your setup. | Was the difference between arranging the space and changing how work happens clear? |

Start without reading the detailed guides. If you need help, use the [Quickstart](QUICKSTART.md) and mention where you needed it. You can stop at any time; unfinished tasks are useful feedback too.

## Tell us what happened

1. State your goal and the actions you tried.
2. Name the point where you paused, guessed, needed help or stopped.
3. Say whether you finished on your own, with help, or did not finish.
4. Describe what you expected to see instead. Include your browser/device if known.

A short account of one confusing moment is more useful than a score alone. Timing is optional. Screenshots or recordings are optional; review them for private information before attaching them to a public issue.

Use [first-use feedback](https://github.com/berkebasbay/loopset/issues/new?template=first_use_feedback.md) for clarity and discoverability, or a [bug report](https://github.com/berkebasbay/loopset/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) for reproducible wrong behavior. Report security concerns through [Security](../SECURITY.md).

## Reading feedback

Distinguish observed behavior from a suggested solution. Include the application revision and the task when comparing results across versions. Browser emulation and automated checks cannot replace accounts from people trying the product for the first time.
